Questions we get asked before anyone pays

Straight answers. If yours is not here, message us on WhatsApp and a person will reply.

How much does an online store cost in Nigeria?

A brochure site with no cart goes for around ₦25,000 and cannot take a payment. A bespoke agency build starts around ₦150,000. Ours sits between the two and actually processes money — see the pricing page for the current figure.

Do I need my own Paystack account?

You need a Nigerian business bank account, and we open the Paystack subaccount against it during setup. It is registered in your name, so your settlements are yours and we cannot touch them.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes. Every store gets a free address like yourshop.[brand].com straight away, and you can point a .com or .com.ng at it whenever you buy one. Nothing about your store changes when you do.

How long does setup take?

Send your product list, photos, delivery areas and bank details and the store is live in a few days. If your catalogue is already in a spreadsheet, we import it in one go rather than typing it in.

What if someone pays and the item has finished?

The payment is never dropped — it is recorded and flagged in your dashboard so you can refund or send a replacement. Losing a real payment because of a stock mistake is the one thing this must never do.

Can I keep selling on Instagram and WhatsApp?

Of course, and most sellers do. The store becomes the link in your bio: you keep posting, and the paying, the delivery fee and the receipt stop happening in your DMs.
